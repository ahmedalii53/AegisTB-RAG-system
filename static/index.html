About AegisTB

Clinical Decision Support System for Tuberculosis

🎯 Overview & Objective

AegisTB is an AI-powered clinical decision-support platform designed to assist healthcare professionals in diagnosing and treating tuberculosis with 100% evidence-grounded accuracy strictly adhering to official World Health Organization (WHO) guidelines.

⚙️ Technical Architecture & Pipeline

Retrieval → Hybrid (Dense + BM25) → RRF Fusion → Evidence Selection → Gemini 2.5 Flash → JSON Validation → Interactive PDF Highlighting
🛡️ Zero-Hallucination Grounding

Gemini is strictly used to synthesize retrieved WHO guidance without fabricating medical doses or unsubstantiated facts.

📑 Exact Document Citations

Every clinical answer maps directly to specific document sections and 1-indexed pages with live PDF sentence highlighting.

🧬 Molecular Diagnostics (mWRD)

Full support for rapid NAATs (Loopamp MTBC, GeneXpert) and urine biomarker testing (LF-LAM) in PLHIV.

💊 Standard & Short Regimens

Covers both 6-month (2HRZE/4HR) and 4-month (2HPMZ/2HPM) drug-susceptible tuberculosis treatment pathways.

📚 Indexed WHO Guideline Corpus

  • WHO Consolidated Guidelines on Tuberculosis — Module 3: Diagnosis (Rapid molecular diagnostics, NAATs, LF-LAM).
  • WHO Consolidated Guidelines on Tuberculosis — Module 4: Treatment and Care (Drug-susceptible TB, 6-month & 4-month regimens, monitoring).
